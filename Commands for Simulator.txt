Commands to execute telemetry_sim in visual code:
	gcc telemetry_sim.c -o telemetry_sim

	./telemetry_sim


temperature_c (Environmental / Equipment Thermal State)
	Corning: Optical laser diode enclosure temperature (lasers degrade if overheated)
	GE Vernova: Transformer core or turbine bearing temperature.
	Duke Energy: Outdoor substation cabinet internal temperature.

voltage_kv (Electrical Power Grid State):
	GE Vernova: Power generation output voltage.
	Duke Energy: Distribution grid line voltage (e.g., monitoring a $12\text{ kV}$ distribution line).
	Corning: Clean backup power feed voltage supplying the fiber optic amplifiers.

optical_loss_db (Signal Line Integrity)
	Corning: Fiber optic cable attenuation (measured in decibels, $\text{dB}$). Signal drops if a cable is bent, damaged, or spliced poorly.
	GE Vernova & Duke Energy: Modern power grids run fiber optics along power lines (known as OPGW—Optical Ground Wire) to transmit grid protection control signals instantly. High optical loss means communications are degrading.

vibration_g (Mechanical & Physical Integrity)
	GE Vernova: Vibration forces ($g$-force) on rotating generator shafts or cooling fans.
	Duke Energy: Transformer vault hum and physical mechanical stress.
	Corning: Structural stability of outdoor fiber equipment racks.

Simulated output in a form:
{
  "device_id": 101,
  "temperature_c": 48.52,
  "voltage_kv": 12.15,
  "optical_loss_db": 0.42,
  "vibration_g": 0.08
}
	

Typical Normal vs. Danger Ranges for Your C Randomizer
	temperature_c: Normal is $35.0$ to $65.0^\circ\text{C}$. Danger (Alert) is anything above $80.0^\circ\text{C}$.

	voltage_kv: Normal is $11.8$ to $12.2\text{ kV}$. Danger is below $11.2\text{ kV}$ (undervoltage) or above $12.8\text{ kV}$ (overvoltage).

	optical_loss_db: Normal is $0.1$ to $1.0\text{ dB}$. Danger is anything above $3.0\text{ dB}$ (signal fade).

	vibration_g: Normal is $0.01$ to $0.15\text{ g}$. Danger is anything above $0.50\text{ g}$ (excessive mechanical shaking).
